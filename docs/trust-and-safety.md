# Trust and Safety

**Status:** Beta
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

---

## Intended use

Premedice is an evidence-first medical information and decision-support assistant for patients, caregivers, and clinicians.

It is not:

- a diagnosis
- a prescription or dose authorization
- a telehealth service
- an emergency service
- a replacement for a licensed clinician
- a guarantee of accuracy

## Emergency and crisis

Premedice is not for emergency care. Chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding, anaphylaxis, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, or suicidal thoughts require local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.

## Evidence checks

Premedice captures sources, shows citation chips, and runs a post-answer evidence check. These are safety aids, not guarantees. The verifier can time out, miss a red flag, or accept a weak source. A missing warning is not proof that an answer is safe.

## Doctor sharing

A shared record is not a clinical endorsement.

- Sharing is patient-controlled and scoped.
- Access can be revoked.
- Doctor access is logged.
- Professional-email verification and signed clinical review are still being expanded.
- Do not treat an unverified account as a verified clinician.

## Data safety

- Do not submit PHI without an appropriate agreement.
- Do not put patient data in GitHub issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Do not paste patient records into public evaluation cases.
- See the privacy policy for retention and residency details.

## Reporting a problem

For security issues, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

For a product safety issue, include the request type, whether a red flag was missed, whether a source was wrong, and a redacted example. Do not include patient identifiers.

## Claims

No clinical accuracy or safety claim is valid until the evaluation is published. See [clinical-evaluation.md](clinical-evaluation.md).
