# What Premedice Delivers

**Status:** Beta
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

---

## The result

Premedice turns a scattered medical record into a cited answer that shows what changed and what needs attention.

**The core result:** earlier warning tracking across labs, medicines, symptoms and history.

---

## Available now

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| Earlier warning tracking | Follows changes across labs, medicines, symptoms and history, not just a single result. |
| Cited answers | Answers arrive with sources attached. |
| Whole-record reasoning | Labs, medicines, documents, symptoms and history are considered together. |
| Clinician-ready summaries | The active record becomes a focused summary for a visit or review. |
| Plain-language explanations | Labs, medicines and reports are explained without jargon. |
| Drug and pharmacogenomic review | Medication interactions and pharmacogenomic notes are surfaced with the record. |
| Guideline and trial lookup | Conditions, guidelines and trials can be researched with citations. |
| Rare-disease matching | Phenotype matching across HPO, Orphanet and MONDO. |
| API and MCP | One OpenAI-compatible API for chat, labs and evidence, plus MCP access. |

---

## Coverage

- 45+ live clinical sources and tools
- 76 active clinical skills
- 54 common lab markers with trends and reference checks
- Labs, medicines, documents, symptoms, timeline and notes

---

## Who it is for

- Patients managing a complex or changing record.
- Caregivers coordinating care for someone else.
- Clinicians reviewing a patient's record before or after a visit.
- Developers building patient-education and clinical-support products.

---

## Safety boundaries

- Premedice is decision support, not a diagnosis.
- It does not replace a licensed clinician.
- Emergency symptoms require local emergency services.
